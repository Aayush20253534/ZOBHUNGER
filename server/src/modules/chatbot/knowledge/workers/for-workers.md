# ZOBHUNGER for Workers

New workers start by submitting a public profile and CV for review. Submitting a profile does **not** automatically create a worker account or guarantee a role. ZOBHUNGER reviews and verifies required details, approves suitable profiles for further consideration, and contacts approved workers when their profile fits a relevant project or role.

## Work categories

- **Sales jobs:** Field sales, customer outreach and territory support.
- **Promoter jobs:** Product demonstrations, in-store activity and campaigns.
- **Field jobs:** Outlet visits, retail audits and work on the ground.
- **Marketing jobs:** Brand activity, customer engagement and market execution.
- **Telecalling jobs:** Customer conversations, lead follow-ups and phone support.
- **Recruitment jobs:** Candidate coordination, screening and interview support.
- **Operations jobs:** Back-office tasks, data work and everyday business support.

## Profile-first onboarding

1. **Submit your profile:** contact details, education, experience, skills, preferred work and CV. No account is required to submit.
2. **Team review and verification:** submitted information is reviewed against suitable workforce/project requirements.
3. **Approval and fit:** role, location, availability and project fit are considered.
4. **Communication and next steps:** when a relevant requirement exists, ZOBHUNGER contacts approved workers and proceeds with onboarding, assignment and access as required.

Existing approved workers can use the authorised worker sign-in. New workers should begin with the public profile submission flow.
