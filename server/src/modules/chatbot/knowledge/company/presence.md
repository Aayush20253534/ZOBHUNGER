# ZOBHUNGER Presence

ZOBHUNGER says its current execution footprint is anchored in **India**, while client relationships may extend worldwide. The public Presence page is about operating coordination, not a promise that every location has the same capacity.

## Public operating locations

- **Headquarters – Ghazipur, Uttar Pradesh:** The central coordination point for workforce deployment, field execution and business support.
- **Branch presence – Delhi:** Supporting requirements across an important northern business and workforce market.
- **Branch presence – Mumbai, Maharashtra:** Extending ZOBHUNGER's execution capability into one of India's largest commercial markets.
- **Branch presence – Bihar:** Strengthening regional workforce and field execution support across eastern markets.
- **Branch presence – Bengaluru, Karnataka:** Supporting workforce, sales and execution requirements across a key southern business market.

The head office contact address is **Vijay Tower, Ghazipur, Uttar Pradesh 233001**.

## How location connects to delivery

The Presence page describes three stages: requirement mapping, regional coordination and on-ground execution. It also summarises the flow as **Business requirement → Regional coordination → Field deployment → Execution & reporting**.

A business can share a market, workforce requirement and objective so the team can map the appropriate coordination path. For a new requirement, use `/hire-workforce` or `/contact`.
