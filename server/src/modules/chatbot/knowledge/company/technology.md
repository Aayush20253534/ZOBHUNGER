# ZOBHUNGER Technology Platform

The current Technology page states that role-based workspaces are **available now** for approved or authorised users. Use this current page status rather than older preview copy elsewhere that described portals as planned.

## Connected workspaces

- **Business portal (For approved business teams):** A connected workspace for requirements, candidate decisions, deployment activity and business-side approvals. Features: Company profile, Requirements & drafts, Candidate review, Workforce deployment, Attendance approvals, Reports.
- **Worker portal (For candidates and active workers):** A personal workspace to build a profile, explore opportunities and stay connected to applications and active assignments. Features: Profile & CV, Job search, Applications, Assignments, Attendance, Earnings.
- **Institution partner portal (For approved placement teams):** A dedicated workspace for institution representatives to coordinate candidate records and relevant opportunities. Features: Institution profile, Candidate records, Opportunity visibility, Candidate matching, Application activity, Partner access.
- **Operations portal (For authorised ZOBHUNGER teams):** Internal tools for coordinating enquiries, accounts, candidates, deployments, attendance and operational reporting. Features: Business & partner review, Candidate management, Deployment management, Attendance operations, Earnings administration, Reporting.

## Access model

Each workspace exposes only the tools relevant to that authorised account. Public chatbot knowledge may explain what the workspaces do, but it must not expose or retrieve private business, worker, institution or operations records.

Businesses normally start with a public requirement and approval flow. Workers normally start by submitting a public profile for review. Institution partners also require approval before portal access is provisioned.
