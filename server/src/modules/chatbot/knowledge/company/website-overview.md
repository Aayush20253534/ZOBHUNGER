# ZOBHUNGER Website Overview

ZOBHUNGER is an integrated workforce, sales and business execution platform. Its public tagline is **Hire. Deploy. Deliver.**. The website positions ZOBHUNGER as an India-based workforce and field-execution partner that helps businesses build and deploy workforce, sales, promoter and field teams around real operating requirements.

## Engagement models

- Permanent: build a core team.
- Contract: support a defined period.
- Project-based: deliver a specific brief.
- On-demand: plan for changing needs.

## Public service catalogue

- **Workforce:** Recruitment and staffing for permanent, contract and project roles. Main page: `/workforce-solutions`.
- **Sales Force:** Build field sales, telesales and business development teams. Main page: `/sales-force`.
- **Telecaller & Telesales:** Trained telecallers for telesales, lead generation, customer engagement and business conversion. Main page: `/telecaller-telesales-services`.
- **Promoters:** People to represent your brand in stores, at events and on campaigns. Main page: `/promoter-solutions`.
- **Retail Execution:** Support merchandising, store audits and product availability checks. Main page: `/retail-execution`.
- **Brand Activation:** Execute branding, advertising, sticker deployment, flyer distribution and consumer campaigns. Main page: `/brand-activation`.
- **Operations:** Recruit teams for customer support, telecalling and back-office work. Main page: `/business-operations`.
- **Gig Workforce:** Find people for short assignments, seasonal demand and project work. Main page: `/gig-workforce`.
- **Verification:** Reliable checks for candidates, employees, documents, businesses and on-ground information. Main page: `/verification-services`.
- **Web & App Development:** Design and build business websites, web platforms, mobile applications and API integrations. Main page: `/website-application-development`.

## Current public scale figures

The homepage scale section lists:

- **15,000+ Gig Field Force** for on-ground execution.
- **70+ clients across industries.**
- **12,000+ deployment PIN codes** covered across India.
- **5 regional offices.**
- A **100% in-house technology platform**, described as powered by AI, Image Recognition, Real-Time Analytics and Business Intelligence tools.
- Sectors listed in that scale section: FMCG, FMEG, Alcobev, FMCD, CPG, E-commerce, FinTech, Healthcare, Banking, Retail, Manufacturing & Other Industries.

These are public editorial figures supplied in the website content. Do not turn them into additional performance claims or guarantees.

## How ZOBHUNGER describes its approach

The site emphasises connected execution, multiple service models, market-led coordination, requirement-led hiring, on-ground coordination and structured reporting. Businesses can start at `/hire-workforce`; workers can start at `/for-workers` or `/jobs`.
