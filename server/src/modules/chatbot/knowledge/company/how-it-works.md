# How ZOBHUNGER Works

ZOBHUNGER starts with a clear business requirement and connects sourcing, selection, deployment, tracking and reporting around that brief.

## Delivery journey

1. **Client shares requirement:** Start with the work to be done. Define the roles, team size, locations, project duration and expected start date. Checkpoint: A clear requirement brief.
2. **ZOBHUNGER sources candidates:** Candidate sourcing is shaped around the role requirements, location and type of engagement agreed for the project. Checkpoint: Candidates aligned with the brief.
3. **Screening and selection:** Review skills, suitability and availability. Agree how candidates will be evaluated and where your team needs to be involved. Checkpoint: Selection criteria and team confirmation.
4. **Training and deployment:** Prepare the selected team for the assignment. Coordinate role briefings, product knowledge, joining details and deployment locations. Checkpoint: A team ready for the assignment.
5. **Attendance and performance tracking:** Agree the attendance records, activity updates and performance measures relevant to the work, together with who reviews them. Checkpoint: An agreed set of work updates.
6. **Reporting and management:** Review progress against the brief, discuss operational needs and plan changes to the team or scope as the project develops. Checkpoint: Reporting and a plan for the next stage.

## What helps shape the plan

- **The work:** Roles, responsibilities, skills and the outcome you want.
- **The locations:** Cities, outlets or territories, with the team size for each.
- **The timeline:** Your expected start date, duration and any rollout stages.
- **The operating plan:** Working hours, supervision and the updates you need.

The exact scope, timeline, supervision, attendance/performance tracking and reporting arrangements are agreed for each assignment. The process should not be described as an automatic guarantee of hiring or deployment.
