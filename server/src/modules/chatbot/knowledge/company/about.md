# About ZOBHUNGER

## Company identity

ZOBHUNGER, also presented as **Zobhungr Solutions Private Limited**, describes itself as a workforce and business execution partner. ZOBHUNGER is an integrated workforce, sales and business execution platform that helps businesses hire, deploy and coordinate teams around real operating requirements.

Its mission is: **Make workforce management faster, smarter and more scalable.**

The company story says the business grew from a belief that opportunity should reach people where they are, reducing unnecessary migration by connecting local talent with work closer to home. The story traces this idea to employment uncertainty and migration during the COVID-19 crisis.

## Operating principles

- **Start with the requirement:** Understand the work, the location and the team before choosing a solution.
- **Connect hiring with execution:** Think beyond filling a role to how the team will be deployed, supported and managed.
- **Make progress visible:** Agree responsibilities and useful reporting around the assignment, with connected tools that keep requirements, people and progress visible.

## Execution model

The About page describes a connected path: **Requirement → Planning → Workforce → Deployment → Execution → Measurement**. Hiring is treated as part of a wider delivery process rather than the end point.

## Core capabilities

The public site lists manpower deployment, field audit, sampling and activation, lead generation, telecaller and telesales services, consumer engagement, and seller onboarding and training among its core capabilities.

## Local-opportunity vision

The company story highlights training, practical guidance and skill development; opportunities closer to a worker's city, district or state; and technology used to connect requirements, talent and assignments while keeping human support central.

The About story also contains a **400+ workers** growing-network statement. The homepage separately lists a **15,000+ Gig Field Force**. Treat those as distinct labels from different public sections and do not assume they measure the same population.
