# Hire Workforce Requirement Form

The public Hire Workforce form is the main detailed starting point for a business requirement. It can be submitted before a business account exists.

## Information requested

### Company and contact
- Company name.
- Contact person.
- Business email.
- Mobile number, with country code recommended.

### Service and team
- Industry.
- Main service required.
- Total number of people across locations, from 1 up to the form limit of 100,000.
- Project duration, such as one day, three months or ongoing.

### Locations and timeline
- One or more job locations. The form supports multiple locations and asks for one location per row.
- Expected start date is optional.
- If several locations are used, the business should explain the team split in the requirement details.

### Requirement details
Describe the roles, responsibilities, skills, outcome, operating arrangement and other information needed to understand the assignment.

## Useful brief checklist
- The work: roles, responsibilities, skills and desired outcome.
- The locations: cities, outlets or territories and team size for each.
- The timeline: expected start date, duration and rollout stages.
- The operating plan: working hours, supervision and required updates.

If the business is still deciding what it needs, the Contact page can be used for a shorter enquiry. For mixed services, choose the main service and explain the additional work in the details.
