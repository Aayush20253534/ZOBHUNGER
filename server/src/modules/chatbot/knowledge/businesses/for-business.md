# ZOBHUNGER for Businesses

New businesses can submit a workforce or execution requirement without first creating a business account. ZOBHUNGER reviews and verifies the required business and project information, then communicates next steps after approval according to the project requirement.

## Starting points

- **Need workforce?** Recruitment and staffing for ongoing roles, seasonal demand or a defined project. Start with the people and skills your business needs. Examples: Recruitment, Contract staffing, Temporary teams. Relevant page: `/workforce-solutions`.
- **Need sales teams?** Build a sales and lead generation team around your product, customer and territory, with clear responsibilities for each role. Examples: Field sales, Lead generation, Telecalling. Relevant page: `/sales-force`.
- **Need promoters?** Bring your product closer to customers with in-store promoters, product demonstrations and campaign support. Examples: In-store promoters, Product demos, Campaign teams. Relevant page: `/promoter-solutions`.
- **Need market execution?** Coordinate the work that happens in outlets and in the field, from merchandising and retail audits to trade marketing activity. Examples: Merchandising, Retail audits, Trade marketing. Relevant page: `/retail-execution`.
- **Need reliable verification?** Review people, documents, business details and field information with a clear verification checklist and documented findings. Examples: Candidate checks, KYC & business, Field verification. Relevant page: `/verification-services`.
- **Need your brand on the ground?** Bring branding and advertising into local markets with sticker deployment, flyer distribution and promotional campaigns. Examples: Sticker deployment, Flyer distribution, Brand activation. Relevant page: `/brand-activation`.
- **Need a website or application?** Turn a business idea into a responsive website, web platform or mobile application with UX/UI, backend, API and launch support. Examples: Business websites, Web applications, Mobile applications. Relevant page: `/website-application-development`.

## Public onboarding flow

1. **Share your requirement:** roles, team size, locations, timeline and work to be completed.
2. **Review and verification:** ZOBHUNGER reviews business and project details and evaluates the service/execution fit.
3. **Approval and next steps:** approved businesses are contacted; approved users can then manage ongoing work in the Business Portal.

## What approved business users can manage

The public page describes company details, requirements, candidate review, workforce deployments, attendance approvals and reports in the authorised Business Portal.

## Practical questions

- **Can one requirement include more than one location?** Yes. Add each location in the requirement form and use the details field to explain the team size or work needed at each one.
- **What if I need a mix of services?** Choose the main service in the form, then describe the other roles or activities in your brief. You can also use the enquiry form if you are still deciding.
- **Do I need to know every detail before starting?** Share what you know about the roles, locations and duration. The expected start date is optional, and you can explain any open questions in the requirement details.

A business account is therefore not required merely to submit the first public requirement. Account access is for approved business users.
