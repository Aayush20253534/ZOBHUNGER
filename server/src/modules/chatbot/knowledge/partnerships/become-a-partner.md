# Independent Business Partner Program

Join a network of experienced professionals, industry experts, consultants and business specialists who want to contribute their expertise while maintaining their professional independence.

## Who the program is for

- Industry Experts
- Business Development Professionals
- Consultants
- Sales & Marketing Professionals
- Recruitment & HR Experts
- Operations Professionals
- Technology Experts
- Project Management Professionals
- Entrepreneurs & Business Owners
- Domain Specialists

## Ways partners may contribute

- Bring business opportunities
- Introduce potential clients
- Share industry expertise
- Support project planning
- Help in business expansion
- Develop new markets
- Build strategic partnerships
- Support sales & marketing
- Recruitment & workforce solutions
- Operations & project execution support

## Flexibility described by the program

- Continue your existing job or business
- Work from anywhere
- No exclusive employment requirement
- Contribute based on your specialization
- Flexible involvement based on availability
- Access Pan-India business opportunities
- Build long-term professional relationships

## Commercial models named on the page

- Revenue Share
- Project-Based Profit Share
- Commission
- Success Fee

These are named collaboration models, not a guarantee of payment amount or project availability.

## Application and approval process

1. **Apply:** Submit your business details, professional profile and partnership interests.
2. **Company review:** Our team evaluates your application and contacts you if more information is needed.
3. **Approval & Partner ID:** After approval, your account is created and a unique Partner ID and temporary password are issued.
4. **Your first login:** Sign in, change the temporary password and open your Business Portal workspace.

## Application information

The public form asks for name, contact details, city, profession, company/business, experience, specialization, industry experience, preferred contribution and partnership area, expertise, and optional professional-network information. A LinkedIn URL is optional. A professional profile/resume PDF is optional and must be 2 MB or smaller.

After approval, the public flow says a Partner ID and temporary password are issued and the partner must change the temporary password on first sign-in.
