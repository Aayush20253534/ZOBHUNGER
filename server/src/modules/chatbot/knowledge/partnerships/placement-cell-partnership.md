# Placement Cell and Institution Partnership

ZOBHUNGER partners with colleges, universities, training institutes and placement teams to connect students with jobs, internships, apprenticeships, freelance work, project work and other flexible opportunities.

## Partnership process

1. **Register Your Institution:** College, University or Institution submits its details through the onboarding process.
2. **Get Portal Login Access:** After approval, the institution receives dedicated access to the ZOBHUNGER Institution Partner Portal.
3. **Upload Candidate Details:** Authorized representatives can add candidate qualification, skills, interests, location and availability.
4. **Match Candidates With Opportunities:** Candidates can be considered for relevant opportunities based on their profile and opportunity requirements.

Availability depends on candidate fit, location, skills, requirements and active projects.

## Earn-while-learning options described on the page

- Task-based assignments
- Freelance projects
- Project-based work
- Payment based on completed and approved work

Payments or weekly payouts are only where applicable and depend on completed and approved work/project requirements.

## Institution Partner Portal

After approval, authorised institution representatives can receive portal access. Publicly listed features include: Upload candidate details, Add multiple student profiles, View available opportunities, Track candidate applications, Manage candidate records, Update candidate information, Check opportunity status, View relevant job and internship opportunities.

## Onboarding form

The public institution form asks for institution name/type, placement or career-services name, optional website, city/state, authorised contact name and designation, official email, mobile number, number of students, courses/departments, and preferred opportunity types. Submitting the form does not itself create portal access; approval is required first.
