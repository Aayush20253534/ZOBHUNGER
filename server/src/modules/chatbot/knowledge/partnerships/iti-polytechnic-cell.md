# ITI and Polytechnic College Cell

The ZOBHUNGER ITI & Polytechnic College Cell is a dedicated technical hiring and institute-partnership channel. It is separate from the general Placement Cell & Institution Partnership.

The general Placement Cell supports broader institutional hiring and multiple educational streams. The ITI & Polytechnic College Cell is specifically focused on ITI trades, Diploma and Polytechnic branches, skilled technical candidates, technical recruitment, apprenticeships and industry-aligned training.

## Who can partner

- Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs)
- Polytechnic colleges
- Other recognised technical institutes

The public onboarding process captures institute type, ownership, affiliation or approval body, location, authorised placement or training contact, student strength, final-year student count, passing batch, trades or branches and partnership interests.

## Student opportunities

Students from associated institutions may be considered for:

- Jobs
- Internships
- Apprenticeships
- Training programs

Opportunity availability depends on active requirements, qualification, trade or branch, skills, location, eligibility and employer selection criteria. Partnership or registration does not guarantee an opportunity.

## Additional partnership areas

Institutes can also indicate interest in campus hiring, technical recruitment drives, industry visits and skill-development programs.

## Technical disciplines

The page gives examples including Electrician, Fitter, Welder, Electronics, Mechanical, Civil, Automobile, Computer Science, Electrical, Electronics & Communication and Industrial Maintenance. Institutes can submit their actual available trades or branches in the onboarding form.

## Partnership process

1. The institute submits its technical partnership profile.
2. ZOBHUNGER reviews the institute and authorised contact information.
3. Trades, branches and student strength are mapped for technical sourcing.
4. Suitable institutes and students can be connected to future requirements based on qualification and eligibility.

The public onboarding route is `/iti-polytechnic-cell/apply`.

## Technical student onboarding

Students from approved ITI, Polytechnic and technical-institute partners can register through `/iti-polytechnic-cell/student-registration`. Registration requires the institute partnership code issued after ZOBHUNGER approves the institute. Student profiles capture qualification, ITI trade or Polytechnic branch, passing year, academic score, skills, certifications, current location, preferred locations and interest in jobs, internships, apprenticeships or training.

Self-registered student profiles enter a pending-verification queue. ZOBHUNGER administrators can verify or deactivate those records. Administrators can also add students manually or bulk-onboard institute rosters from `.xlsx` or `.csv` files. Bulk files are validated row by row, and duplicate email or enrollment records are skipped rather than silently overwritten.

Student registration creates an eligibility profile only. It does not guarantee a job, internship, apprenticeship or training seat. Matching depends on active requirements, institute partnership status, student qualification and skills, location, employer criteria and other eligibility conditions.
