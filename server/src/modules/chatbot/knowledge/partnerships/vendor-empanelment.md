# Vendor Empanelment and Onboarding

ZOBHUNGER invites companies, agencies, MSMEs, startups and specialist service providers to apply to its vendor network. Company review occurs before empanelment, and project opportunities depend on project fit, capability and availability.

## Collaboration areas

- **Manpower & Workforce Solutions:** deployment, staffing and flexible workforce capacity.
- **Recruitment & Talent Sourcing:** candidate sourcing, screening and recruitment support.
- **Advertising & Marketing:** branding, advertising, campaigns and market activation.
- **Business & Operational Support:** customer support, field work and business processes.
- **Other Specialized Services:** expert capabilities shaped around client requirements.

## Empanelment path

1. Introduce the company, services, experience, coverage and company profile.
2. ZOBHUNGER reviews and evaluates the application and may follow up.
3. Approved vendors receive a Vendor Code and can be considered for suitable projects.

## Application information

The vendor form asks for company/legal name, organisation type, business address, city, state/region, postal code, country, authorised contact, designation, business email and phone. Website/portfolio URL is optional.

Applicants select at least one service area and describe services/capabilities, years of relevant experience, team size/workforce capacity, service locations, industries served, availability/delivery capacity, relevant projects and optional notable clients/reference work.

## Supporting documents

A **company profile/capability deck PDF is required**. Business registration, GST/tax document, MSME/Udyam certificate and other supporting documents are optional where applicable. The public form limits each PDF to **2 MB** and one file per document category.

Empanelment means inclusion in the vendor network; it does not guarantee project allocation.
