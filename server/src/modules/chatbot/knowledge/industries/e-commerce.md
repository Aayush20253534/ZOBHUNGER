# E-Commerce Industry Solutions

## Overview

Build customer support, data and back-office capacity around your e-commerce workflow. Plan ongoing teams or additional help for a defined campaign or seasonal workload.

Customer support, data work and operations teams for everyday workflows and seasonal requirements.

## Operating context

- **Work settings:** Support desks, back offices & remote teams
- **Team focus:** Customer enquiries & operational tasks
- **Planning starts with:** Workload, responsibilities & engagement dates

## Services for this industry

Choose the roles that match your customer conversations, records and operational tasks.

- **Customer support:** Recruit people for the customer queries and support activities defined in your workflow. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Telecalling:** Coordinate enquiry follow-ups and structured customer calling around an agreed brief. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Data collection:** Gather the records or responses your operational process requires. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Data verification:** Review information against the checks and criteria set by your team. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Back office support:** Add capacity for record handling, administrative tasks and routine operations. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Seasonal operations workforce:** Plan additional staffing for a sales campaign, seasonal period or defined operations project. Relevant service: `/gig-workforce`.

## Common scenarios

- **Customer enquiry coverage:** Define the customer questions, working hours and hand-offs your support team needs to cover.
- **A time-bound data project:** Set the records, review steps and project duration before arranging the people doing the work.
- **Campaign support:** Prepare extra operations capacity around a known promotion window or seasonal requirement.

## What to include in a requirement

Describe the workflow and the responsibilities the new team will own.

- **Functions & tasks:** Explain the customer support, calling, data or back-office work involved.
- **Team size & workload:** Share the number of roles and the workload to plan around.
- **Working arrangement:** Specify location or remote requirements, hours and assignment duration.
- **Process & hand-offs:** Define task instructions, review points and the updates your team needs.
