# Manufacturing Industry Solutions

## Overview

Coordinate recruitment, contract staffing and business support around your work locations. Define the roles, experience and engagement schedule before building the team.

Permanent recruitment, contract staffing and operational support for your manufacturing business.

## Operating context

- **Work settings:** Work sites, offices & support operations
- **Team focus:** Defined role requirements & staffing plans
- **Planning starts with:** Skills, work locations & engagement period

## Services for this industry

Match the hiring model and operational roles to the requirements of the assignment.

- **Permanent recruitment:** Source and screen candidates against the responsibilities and experience your roles require. Relevant service: `/workforce-solutions`.
- **Contract staffing:** Build a team around a specified role scope and engagement period. Relevant service: `/workforce-solutions`.
- **Bulk hiring:** Coordinate sourcing and screening when several people are needed across similar roles. Relevant service: `/workforce-solutions`.
- **Temporary workforce:** Plan additional people for an agreed workload period or project requirement. Relevant service: `/gig-workforce`.
- **Data verification:** Support operational record checks against the process defined by your team. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Back office support:** Build capacity for administrative responsibilities and routine business operations. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.

## Common scenarios

- **Several roles to fill:** Group the roles, skills and joining requirements into a coordinated hiring brief.
- **A defined project period:** Set the locations, headcount and engagement dates for a contract or temporary requirement.
- **Business support capacity:** Identify record handling and office responsibilities that need an additional support team.

## What to include in a requirement

Specific role and worksite requirements help focus the candidate search and deployment plan.

- **Roles & experience:** Provide the job responsibilities, experience and skills required for selection.
- **Work locations:** Share the site or office locations and role-specific working arrangements.
- **Headcount & engagement:** Specify team sizes, shifts, duration and the expected joining schedule.
- **Onboarding needs:** Explain the preparation, supervision and reporting arrangements for the roles.

Share the roles, locations, headcount and engagement schedule. We’ll help you plan the hiring work. The page action is **Build your staffing requirement**.
