# Telecom Industry Solutions

## Overview

Coordinate sales, promotions and outreach around your markets and channels. Build field and support roles that match your customer conversations and coverage plan.

Field sales, promoters, customer outreach and support teams for your telecom requirements.

## Operating context

- **Work settings:** Sales territories, outlets & calling teams
- **Team focus:** Customer reach & product conversations
- **Planning starts with:** Markets, channels & role requirements

## Services for this industry

Bring the field, promotion and support activities into one clear team plan.

- **Field sales executives:** Support customer visits and sales activity across assigned locations or territories. Relevant service: `/sales-force`.
- **Sales promoters:** Arrange people to explain your offering and assist customer conversations at selected locations. Relevant service: `/promoter-solutions`.
- **Telecalling teams:** Coordinate telephone outreach and enquiry follow-ups around an agreed brief. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Lead generation teams:** Build capacity for identifying prospects and supporting initial outreach. Relevant service: `/sales-force`.
- **Customer support:** Recruit people for the customer queries and support workflows your business defines. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Market surveys:** Collect responses and field observations using your agreed questions and locations. Relevant service: `/retail-execution`.

## Common scenarios

- **Territory coverage:** Define the locations and field sales responsibilities needed to support your coverage plan.
- **A local promotion:** Coordinate promoter roles, product preparation and customer interactions at selected outlets or venues.
- **Outreach support:** Build a calling or support team around your enquiry volumes and follow-up workflow.

## What to include in a requirement

Explain which channels the team will work in and how their activity will be reviewed.

- **Markets & channels:** List the territories, outlets or calling channels you need to cover.
- **Roles & people:** Specify sales, promoter and support roles with the required headcount.
- **Product & activity brief:** Describe the offering and the customer interactions expected from each role.
- **Schedule & updates:** Share assignment dates, working hours and activity reporting needs.
