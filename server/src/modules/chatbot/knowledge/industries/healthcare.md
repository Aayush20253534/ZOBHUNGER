# Healthcare Industry Solutions

## Overview

Build non-clinical enquiry, outreach and back-office capacity around your healthcare organisation. Define the responsibilities and hand-offs for each support role.

Non-clinical enquiry, calling, recruitment and back-office support around your operations.

## Operating context

- **Work settings:** Enquiry desks, offices & remote support roles
- **Team focus:** Non-clinical customer & administrative support
- **Planning starts with:** Role boundaries, workflow & staffing needs

## Services for this industry

Plan enquiry and administrative roles around clear work instructions and hand-offs.

- **Customer enquiry teams:** Recruit people to handle the non-clinical enquiries and support tasks your workflow defines. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Telecalling:** Coordinate structured outreach and follow-up calls against an agreed activity brief. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Support recruitment:** Source candidates for customer support, office coordination and administrative roles. Relevant service: `/workforce-solutions`.
- **Data collection:** Arrange people for the information-gathering tasks defined in your support process. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Data verification:** Support specified record checks using the instructions and review criteria you provide. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Back office teams:** Build capacity for administrative tasks, coordination and routine support work. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.

## Common scenarios

- **Enquiry coverage:** Set the enquiry types, support hours and hand-offs required from the team.
- **An outreach programme:** Define the calling brief, roles and engagement period for a planned outreach activity.
- **Administrative capacity:** Identify the coordination and record-handling tasks that need additional staff.

## What to include in a requirement

Describe the support responsibility, including where the role hands work back to your organisation.

- **Role scope:** Set the enquiry, calling or administrative responsibilities involved.
- **Team & locations:** Share headcount and whether roles are on-site, office-based or remote.
- **Working schedule:** Specify support hours, engagement dates and the joining requirement.
- **Instructions & hand-offs:** Explain the work process, preparation and internal review or escalation points.

## How to start

Tell us the non-clinical roles, locations and schedule. We’ll help you shape the staffing requirement. The page action is **Plan your support workforce**.
