# FMCG Industry Solutions

## Overview

Coordinate promoters, merchandising, field sales and campaign support around your products and outlets. Bring store execution and customer engagement into one workforce plan.

Promoters, merchandising, field sales and activation support for your products and retail coverage.

## Operating context

- **Work settings:** Stores, outlets & campaign locations
- **Team focus:** Product visibility & customer interaction
- **Planning starts with:** Products, coverage & activity schedule

## Services for this industry

Connect store-level work with the sales and campaign activities around it.

- **Sales promoters:** Support product conversations and purchase decisions at the outlets in your promotion plan. Relevant service: `/promoter-solutions`.
- **Merchandisers:** Coordinate product placement and presentation against the shelf and display brief. Relevant service: `/retail-execution`.
- **Field sales teams:** Build a team for assigned outlet visits, customer follow-ups and market coverage. Relevant service: `/sales-force`.
- **Product sampling:** Arrange people for sampling activities that introduce your products to the intended audience. Relevant service: `/brand-activation`.
- **Retail audits:** Collect agreed observations about product availability, presentation and store execution. Relevant service: `/retail-execution`.
- **Brand activations:** Coordinate on-ground teams and promotional activities around your campaign brief. Relevant service: `/brand-activation`.

## Common scenarios

- **A product introduction:** Bring sampling, promoters and demonstrations together around a new product and its target audience.
- **A store execution programme:** Plan merchandising and audit activity across the outlets selected for the programme.
- **Additional market coverage:** Define the territories, outlet visits and field sales roles needed for the next stage of coverage.

## What to include in a requirement

A clear product and outlet brief helps connect the right roles with the right activities.

- **Products & activities:** Name the products and the promotion, sales or merchandising work you need.
- **Outlets & locations:** Share the store list, cities and coverage expected from the team.
- **People & dates:** Specify role counts, campaign dates and any shift requirements.
- **Checks & updates:** Describe the store checklist and reporting you want to review.
