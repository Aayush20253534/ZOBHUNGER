# Startups Industry Solutions

## Overview

Build hiring, sales and business support around the work your startup needs next. Combine ongoing roles with project or remote capacity as your requirements take shape.

Recruitment, remote sales, customer support and project teams for the work your startup needs next.

## Operating context

- **Work settings:** Office, remote & project assignments
- **Team focus:** Hiring, customer outreach & operations
- **Planning starts with:** The next priority, role scope & duration

## Services for this industry

Start with the function that needs support, then choose the roles and engagement model.

- **Permanent recruitment:** Source and screen candidates for the ongoing roles your business wants to build. Relevant service: `/workforce-solutions`.
- **Remote sales teams:** Arrange roles for prospecting, sales conversations and follow-ups suited to remote delivery. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Lead generation:** Build capacity for prospect research, outreach and qualification around your customer brief. Relevant service: `/sales-force`.
- **Customer support:** Recruit people for the customer questions and support workflows you need covered. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Back office support:** Add capacity for record handling, coordination and routine administrative tasks. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Project-based workforce:** Plan people for a campaign, field activity or assignment with a defined scope and duration. Relevant service: `/gig-workforce`.

## Common scenarios

- **An ongoing role:** Turn a recurring responsibility into a clear role brief for recruitment and selection.
- **Customer outreach:** Plan sales and lead generation capacity around the customer segment you want to reach.
- **A defined project:** Arrange a team around the dates, locations and tasks of a specific assignment.

## What to include in a requirement

A focused first brief helps identify the role or combination of services you need.

- **Business priority:** Describe the work to be done and the outcome your team is working towards.
- **Roles & people:** Share the functions, responsibilities and number of people you need.
- **Working arrangement:** Set location or remote needs, working hours and engagement duration.
- **Start & coordination:** Explain the expected start date, preparation and reporting arrangement.
