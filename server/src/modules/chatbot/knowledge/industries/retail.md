# Retail Industry Solutions

## Overview

Plan people and execution around your store network. Connect in-store promotions, merchandising, audits and seasonal staffing with the work each outlet needs.

In-store promoters, merchandising, audits and flexible staffing around your retail operations.

## Operating context

- **Work settings:** Stores, shop floors & outlet networks
- **Team focus:** Store standards & shopper interaction
- **Planning starts with:** Outlet coverage, roles & store schedules

## Services for this industry

Define what each store needs, from customer-facing support to a structured execution checklist.

- **In-store promoters:** Arrange people to explain products and assist shoppers during in-store promotions. Relevant service: `/promoter-solutions`.
- **Merchandising:** Support product arrangement and presentation against the store's merchandising brief. Relevant service: `/retail-execution`.
- **Store audits:** Review agreed store conditions and operational standards using a defined checklist. Relevant service: `/retail-execution`.
- **Mystery audits:** Assess agreed aspects of the shopping experience through planned visits. Relevant service: `/retail-execution`.
- **Product availability checks:** Check whether selected products are available across the outlets in your programme. Relevant service: `/retail-execution`.
- **Seasonal staffing:** Plan additional people for a defined period of store activity or seasonal workload. Relevant service: `/gig-workforce`.

## Common scenarios

- **Store promotions:** Coordinate promoter roles, product preparation and shift coverage for an in-store campaign.
- **Outlet checks:** Build a visit programme around the stores, standards and product checks you want to review.
- **Seasonal demand:** Set the dates and tasks that need additional staffing during a promotion or busy trading period.

## What to include in a requirement

Start with the store list and the work expected at each location.

- **Store coverage:** List the outlets, cities and visit or staffing schedule.
- **Roles & headcount:** Set the number of promoters, support staff or field people required.
- **Product & store brief:** Explain the products, store standards and activities involved.
- **Duration & reporting:** Share shift timings, engagement dates and the updates you need.
