# Consumer Electronics Industry Solutions

## Overview

Build promoter, demonstration and retail execution support around your products. Connect customer interactions with the display, sales and campaign work behind them.

Product demonstrators, in-store promoters, retail checks and sales teams for electronics brands.

## Operating context

- **Work settings:** Retail stores, sales territories & launch venues
- **Team focus:** Product explanation & store execution
- **Planning starts with:** Products, role preparation & outlet coverage

## Services for this industry

Define the product message and the execution standards your store or launch programme needs.

- **Product demonstrators:** Arrange people to explain product features and demonstrate the agreed use cases. Relevant service: `/promoter-solutions`.
- **In-store promoters:** Support shopper questions and product conversations at selected retail locations. Relevant service: `/promoter-solutions`.
- **Visual merchandising:** Coordinate display and product presentation against your merchandising brief. Relevant service: `/retail-execution`.
- **Store audits:** Review agreed display, availability and store execution criteria through planned visits. Relevant service: `/retail-execution`.
- **Territory sales teams:** Build sales coverage around the markets, outlets and customer segments you define. Relevant service: `/sales-force`.
- **Launch campaigns:** Connect promoters and on-ground activities around a product launch programme. Relevant service: `/brand-activation`.

## Common scenarios

- **A product demonstration programme:** Define the features, demonstration brief and store roles needed for your product range.
- **A launch activation:** Coordinate the people, venues and customer interactions planned for a product introduction.
- **A retail standards review:** Build store visits around the presentation and availability checks your team wants to assess.

## What to include in a requirement

Give the team a clear product brief and the coverage required at each location.

- **Products & preparation:** List the products and the knowledge or demonstrations each role needs.
- **Stores & territories:** Share the outlet locations, markets or campaign venues involved.
- **Roles & duration:** Set promoter, sales or field headcount with dates and shifts.
- **Execution standards:** Describe display criteria, visit checklists and the updates you require.
