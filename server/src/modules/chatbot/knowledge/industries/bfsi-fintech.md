# BFSI & Fintech Industry Solutions

## Overview

Plan field sales, lead generation, calling and business support around your customer and operational workflows. Define the role responsibilities before building the team.

Field sales, outreach, telecalling and business operations teams aligned with your workflow.

## Operating context

- **Work settings:** Field territories, support desks & remote teams
- **Team focus:** Customer outreach & operational support
- **Planning starts with:** Role scope, customer segments & work instructions

## Services for this industry

Connect customer-facing roles with the operational support around them.

- **Field sales teams:** Organise customer visits and field activity within the territories defined in your brief. Relevant service: `/sales-force`.
- **Lead generation:** Build capacity for prospect research, outreach and qualification against agreed criteria. Relevant service: `/sales-force`.
- **Telecalling:** Support structured calling and follow-ups using the work instructions you provide. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Customer support:** Recruit people for defined customer queries and support hand-offs. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Data verification:** Arrange people to review information against your specified checks and process. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Back office support:** Build capacity for administrative tasks, records and day-to-day support operations. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.

## Common scenarios

- **Defined market coverage:** Specify the customer segments, territories and outreach responsibilities of the field team.
- **Calling and follow-up capacity:** Plan the people, work instructions and update process needed for a structured calling requirement.
- **Operations support:** Identify the tasks and review steps that require additional back-office capacity.

## What to include in a requirement

A clear role scope helps separate outreach responsibilities from the decisions owned by your business.

- **Roles & responsibilities:** Describe the work each role will do and the hand-offs to your team.
- **Customers & coverage:** Share the segments, locations or outreach channels involved.
- **People & schedule:** Set the team size, working hours and engagement period.
- **Workflow & review:** Provide the task instructions, review criteria and reporting expectations.
