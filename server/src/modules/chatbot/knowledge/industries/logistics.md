# Logistics Industry Solutions

## Overview

Plan operational, field and support roles around the work your logistics business needs done. Build ongoing capacity or arrange extra people for a defined period of activity.

Operations staff, field roles and flexible support teams around your logistics workflow.

## Operating context

- **Work settings:** Operational locations, field assignments & offices
- **Team focus:** Defined tasks & coordinated hand-offs
- **Planning starts with:** Locations, workloads & working schedules

## Services for this industry

Define the assignment and the role requirements before planning the workforce.

- **Operations staff:** Recruit people for the operational responsibilities defined at your work locations. Relevant service: `/workforce-solutions`.
- **Temporary staffing:** Plan additional capacity for a specified workload period or project. Relevant service: `/workforce-solutions`.
- **Field executives:** Arrange people for assigned visits, location-based tasks and field activities. Relevant service: `/gig-workforce`.
- **Data verification:** Support record checks against the criteria and workflow your operations team supplies. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Customer support:** Build a team for defined customer enquiries and support hand-offs. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.
- **Remote operations teams:** Coordinate roles suited to remote data, calling or back-office work. Relevant service: `/business-operations`.

## Common scenarios

- **Additional workload:** Set the roles, locations and engagement period needed for a known increase in activity.
- **A field assignment:** Describe the visits, tasks and geographic coverage the field team will handle.
- **Back-office coordination:** Connect record handling and customer support roles to your operational hand-offs.

## What to include in a requirement

A location and task brief helps organise the people around the operation.

- **Work locations:** Share the sites, offices or field areas where roles are required.
- **Tasks & role criteria:** Describe the responsibilities and experience each role needs.
- **Headcount & timing:** Set team sizes, shifts and the assignment duration.
- **Coordination needs:** Explain reporting, preparation and the hand-offs to your existing team.
