# Food & Beverage Industry Solutions

## Overview

Connect product sampling, promotions and field sales around your brand. Plan the people needed for outlet activity, customer engagement and a defined campaign schedule.

Sampling, promoters, field sales and retail execution support for your food and beverage brand.

## Operating context

- **Work settings:** Retail outlets & agreed campaign venues
- **Team focus:** Product introductions & brand interaction
- **Planning starts with:** Products, venues & campaign instructions

## Services for this industry

Shape the team around the promotion, sales or retail activity you want to deliver.

- **Product sampling:** Coordinate people for sampling activity at the venues and dates in your campaign brief. Relevant service: `/brand-activation`.
- **Brand promoters:** Introduce your brand and support product conversations with the intended audience. Relevant service: `/promoter-solutions`.
- **Field sales teams:** Build a team for outlet visits and sales follow-ups in selected markets. Relevant service: `/sales-force`.
- **Retail audits:** Collect agreed observations about product availability, presentation and outlet activity. Relevant service: `/retail-execution`.
- **Brand activations:** Arrange on-ground campaign support around your product and customer engagement plan. Relevant service: `/brand-activation`.
- **Seasonal support teams:** Plan additional people for a defined promotion window or seasonal campaign. Relevant service: `/gig-workforce`.

## Common scenarios

- **A product sampling campaign:** Set the product brief, venues, dates and promoter roles for an organised sampling activity.
- **Retail visibility work:** Coordinate outlet checks and promotion activity around the locations that matter to your brand.
- **A seasonal activation:** Build a short-term team around a specified campaign calendar and target audience.

## What to include in a requirement

Describe the product, activity and preparations the team needs before deployment.

- **Product & activity:** Explain the sampling, promotion, sales or retail work you need.
- **Venues & audience:** List the outlets or campaign locations and the audience you want to reach.
- **Team & campaign dates:** Share role counts, working schedules and engagement dates.
- **Preparation & updates:** Provide the activity instructions, venue arrangements and reporting expectations.

## How to start

Tell us your product, venues and workforce needs. We’ll help connect the team with the activity. The page action is **Discuss your campaign requirement**.
