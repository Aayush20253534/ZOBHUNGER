## When to use the Contact page

Businesses can use the Contact page for a general question about staffing, sales teams, field/business execution or digital development. If a detailed workforce requirement is already known, `/hire-workforce` is the better path because it collects team size, locations, timeline and responsibilities.

For website/application projects, the contact brief should include what the business wants to build, who will use it, main features, existing systems or integrations, and preferred timeline.

Workers should use `/for-workers`, `/jobs` or `/careers/apply` rather than submitting a business enquiry for a job.
