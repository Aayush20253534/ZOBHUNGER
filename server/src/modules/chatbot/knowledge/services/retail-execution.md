# Retail Execution Services

## What ZOBHUNGER provides

Support what happens on the shelf and the shop floor. Coordinate merchandising, store checks and market observations around the standards your brand needs.

**Best for:** Businesses that need store-level execution, merchandising support or a clearer view of retail conditions.

- **Work setting:** Stores, outlets & retail markets
- **Activities:** Merchandising, audits & market checks
- **Planning focus:** Store list, checklist & reporting scope

## Service scope

Define the stores and the checks that matter, then choose the services your programme needs.

- **Merchandising:** Support the presentation and placement of your products against an agreed store brief.
- **Store audits:** Review store conditions and agreed operational or brand standards using a defined checklist.
- **Retail audits:** Collect structured observations about retail execution across selected outlets.
- **Mystery audits:** Assess agreed aspects of the customer experience through planned store visits.
- **Product availability checks:** Check whether specified products are available at the outlets in your programme.
- **Shelf management:** Support agreed product arrangement, shelf presentation and upkeep activities.
- **Visual merchandising:** Coordinate displays and product presentation against your visual merchandising brief.
- **Competitor analysis:** Collect agreed market observations on competing products, presentation and retail activity.
- **Market surveys:** Gather responses and observations using the questions and locations defined in your brief.

## Planning guidance

**Make the store brief specific.** Clear coverage and checklists make observations easier to review and act on.

- **Store coverage:** List the outlets, locations and visit schedule that your programme should cover.
- **Execution checklist:** Agree the products, display standards and observations that each visit should address.
- **Reporting scope:** Define the records, summaries and update frequency you need from the work.

## Delivery process

Start with the checklist and build the field activity around it.

1. **Scope:** Confirm stores, services, coverage and the programme schedule.
2. **Prepare:** Agree the checklist and brief the execution team.
3. **Execute:** Carry out the assigned merchandising, audits or checks.
4. **Review:** Review observations and discuss the next actions with your team.
