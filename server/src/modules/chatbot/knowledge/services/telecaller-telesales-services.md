# Telecaller & Telesales Services

## What ZOBHUNGER provides

Deploy trained telecallers for telesales, lead generation, customer engagement and business conversion. We build the calling team, prepare the conversation and keep follow-ups moving.

**Best for:** Businesses that need consistent outbound outreach, responsive customer calling or a dedicated telephone sales team.

- **Channels:** Inbound, outbound & follow-up calls
- **Team model:** Dedicated, campaign or project teams
- **Focus:** Qualified conversations & conversion

## Service scope

Choose a focused calling service or combine multiple workstreams into one managed telecaller and telesales team.

- **Telecalling / outbound calling:** Reach prospects and customers with a clear script, approved offer and structured call outcome tracking.
- **Telesales:** Build telephone sales capacity for product conversations, objection handling and order or application conversion.
- **Lead generation:** Research, contact and qualify prospects against your customer profile and hand over actionable leads.
- **Lead follow-up & conversion:** Keep warm leads moving with timely callbacks, context from earlier conversations and clear next actions.
- **Customer calling & support:** Handle customer questions, service callbacks and routine support conversations with a helpful, consistent tone.
- **Sales calling:** Run focused sales campaigns around a product, territory, customer segment or seasonal offer.
- **Appointment / demo booking:** Qualify interest and schedule sales meetings, product demos or service appointments for your team.
- **Verification & feedback calling:** Confirm information, collect structured feedback and flag records that need a human review.
- **Inbound & outbound call handling:** Combine responsive inbound coverage with proactive outbound activity in one coordinated operating model.
- **Dedicated telecaller / telesales manpower:** Add a trained, supervised calling team that works to your hours, tools, reporting format and campaign goals.

## Planning guidance

**Make every call part of a clear customer journey.** The strongest calling programmes align the audience, the conversation and the follow-up before the first dial.

- **Audience & offer:** Define who the team will call, why the conversation matters and what a successful outcome looks like.
- **Script & training:** Prepare the team with product context, call guidance, objection handling and the right escalation path.
- **Follow-up & reporting:** Set outcome codes, callback rules and reporting checkpoints so the team can improve conversion over time.

## Delivery process

A clear operating brief gives the team the context and support needed to have better conversations.

1. **Brief:** Confirm the audience, offer, call volume, working hours and success measures.
2. **Train:** Prepare the team on the product, script, CRM workflow and quality expectations.
3. **Call:** Start inbound, outbound or mixed calling with supervised early conversations.
4. **Follow up:** Prioritise callbacks, appointments and qualified opportunities against the agreed rules.
5. **Review:** Review call outcomes, quality signals and conversion trends with your team.
