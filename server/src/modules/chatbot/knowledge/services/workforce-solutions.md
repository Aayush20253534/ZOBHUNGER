# Workforce Services

## What ZOBHUNGER provides

Hire for permanent roles, contract assignments or a growing project. Bring recruitment, screening and deployment together around your workforce requirement.

**Best for:** Businesses building a new team, filling several roles or planning staffing across locations.

- **Engagement:** Permanent, contract & project roles
- **Team needs:** Blue-collar, white-collar & remote
- **Hiring support:** Individual roles, bulk hiring & RPO

## Service scope

Choose the hiring model that fits the role, duration and size of your requirement.

- **Permanent recruitment:** Source and screen candidates for long-term positions in your business.
- **Contract staffing:** Build a team around a defined assignment, work scope and engagement period.
- **Temporary staffing:** Plan short-term staffing for additional workload, campaigns or temporary requirements.
- **Bulk hiring:** Coordinate sourcing and screening when you need several people across similar roles.
- **Project-based hiring:** Match the team and hiring schedule to a specific project and its delivery needs.
- **Blue collar hiring:** Recruit for on-ground, store and operational roles with clear job requirements.
- **White collar hiring:** Find candidates for sales, support, recruitment and business operations positions.
- **Gig workforce:** Arrange people for short assignments, seasonal demand and defined tasks.
- **Remote workforce:** Build teams for roles that can be delivered remotely with an agreed workflow.
- **Recruitment process outsourcing:** Coordinate an agreed part of your recruitment process, from sourcing through candidate screening and hiring support.

## Planning guidance

**Different roles. One clear hiring brief.** Start with the work to be done and the skills it needs. We’ll use that brief to shape the candidate search.

- **On-ground workforce:** Store, field and operational roles, with location and shift requirements defined up front.
- **Business professionals:** Sales, recruitment, customer support and back-office roles aligned with your team's responsibilities.
- **Remote teams:** Telecalling, recruitment, sales and operations roles suited to a coordinated remote workflow.

## Delivery process

A shared brief keeps sourcing, selection and deployment aligned.

1. **Requirement:** Define roles, headcount, locations, skills and the expected start date.
2. **Sourcing:** Identify candidates against the agreed role requirements.
3. **Screening:** Review suitability, experience and availability for the assignment.
4. **Selection:** Coordinate interviews and confirm the people moving forward.
5. **Deployment:** Prepare the selected team for onboarding and joining.
