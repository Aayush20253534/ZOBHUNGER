# Verification Services

## What ZOBHUNGER provides

We provide reliable and transparent verification services to help businesses make confident decisions. Connect document checks, candidate reviews and on-ground verification in one coordinated workflow.

**Best for:** Businesses reviewing candidates, employees, customers, merchants or field information before the next decision.

- **Coverage:** People, documents, businesses & locations
- **Execution:** Desk-based reviews & on-ground checks
- **Handover:** Findings, evidence & items needing clarification

## Service scope

Choose individual checks or combine them into a workflow tailored to your business requirements.

- **Candidate & Employee Verification:** Review candidate and employee information against the checks agreed for your hiring or workforce programme.
- **Identity & Document Verification:** Check submitted identity details and supporting documents, recording mismatches and missing information.
- **Address Verification:** Coordinate address checks and field visits, with visit observations recorded for your review.
- **Employment & Experience Verification:** Check declared employment history, roles and experience through the agreed verification process.
- **Background Verification:** Bring the selected background checks together in a structured review with clear findings and open items.
- **KYC & Business Verification:** Support customer, merchant and business onboarding with the document and business checks in your brief.
- **Field & On-Ground Verification:** Deploy executives to agreed locations to confirm field information and capture permitted visit evidence.
- **Custom Verification:** Build a verification checklist around your business requirements, locations and reporting needs.

## Planning guidance

**Define the checks. Make the process transparent.** Agree what needs to be verified and how findings will be reviewed before the assignment begins.

- **Scope & authorisation:** Confirm the checks, required permissions, supporting information and people involved.
- **Locations & timelines:** Plan the volume, visit locations, contact arrangements and expected turnaround.
- **Evidence & reporting:** Agree the reporting format, evidence to capture and process for incomplete or conflicting details.

## Delivery process

A consistent process keeps each check connected to its supporting information and next action.

1. **Scope:** Agree the verification checklist, required permissions and reporting needs.
2. **Check:** Review documents, contact the relevant sources and complete agreed field visits.
3. **Review:** Check findings for completeness and flag differences or missing information.
4. **Report:** Share the recorded findings and open items for your team's decision.
