# Web & App Development Services

## What ZOBHUNGER provides

Turn an idea or business requirement into a responsive website, web platform or mobile application. We bring planning, UX/UI, engineering, integrations, testing and launch into one practical delivery flow.

**Best for:** Businesses that need a new website, customer or partner portal, internal web platform, mobile application or API-connected digital product.

- **Builds:** Websites, web apps, mobile apps & APIs
- **Delivery:** UX/UI, engineering, testing & launch
- **Engagement:** New builds, redesigns & integrations

## Service scope

Start with the outcome you need, then choose the right mix of design, frontend, backend, mobile and integration work.

- **Business websites:** Build responsive, fast and professional websites for company presence, services, lead generation and customer communication.
- **Web applications & portals:** Create secure browser-based platforms for customers, partners, teams, workflows, dashboards and operational processes.
- **Mobile application development:** Build practical mobile experiences for Android and iOS around the features, users and workflows your business needs.
- **UI/UX design:** Turn requirements into clear user journeys, wireframes and interfaces that are easy to understand and use.
- **Backend & API development:** Build the server-side logic, databases and APIs that power websites, applications and connected business systems.
- **Third-party integrations:** Connect payments, maps, email, authentication, analytics, cloud services and other APIs required by the product.
- **Redesign, optimisation & support:** Improve an existing digital product with better usability, responsive behaviour, performance and maintainable implementation.

## Planning guidance

**Build the right product before adding more features.** A useful digital project starts with the users, business goal and core workflow. We shape the implementation around those priorities rather than adding technology for its own sake.

- **Business outcome:** Define what the website or application should help the business achieve and how success will be judged.
- **Users & journeys:** Identify the people using the product, the actions they need to complete and the information they need along the way.
- **Technology & integrations:** Choose an architecture that fits the product today while keeping APIs, integrations and future growth practical.

## Delivery process

Each stage produces something reviewable before the project moves forward.

1. **Discover:** Understand the business goal, users, features, integrations and project constraints.
2. **Design:** Shape the information architecture, user journeys and interface before full development.
3. **Build:** Develop the frontend, backend, mobile experience and integrations required by the agreed scope.
4. **Test:** Review functionality, responsive behaviour, performance and important user flows before release.
5. **Launch:** Deploy the product, verify the production setup and plan the next improvements or support requirements.
