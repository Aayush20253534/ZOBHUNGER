# Brand Activation Services

## What ZOBHUNGER provides

Bring your brand into the places your audience visits. Coordinate branding and advertising, sticker deployment, flyer distribution, sampling and promotional campaigns with one execution team.

**Best for:** Brands planning a product introduction, local campaign or direct consumer engagement programme.

- **Channels:** On-ground campaigns & events
- **Venues:** Malls, colleges & campaign locations
- **Team focus:** Sampling, demonstrations & engagement

## Service scope

Build the activation around your audience, venue and the experience you want people to have.

- **Branding & Advertising:** Coordinate local brand visibility and advertising activity around your audience, message and campaign locations.
- **Sticker Deployment:** Place approved stickers at agreed outlets and positions, then record placement and completion details.
- **Flyer/Pamphlet Distribution:** Brief distribution teams, plan routes and introduce your offer to the intended audience.
- **Promotional Campaigns:** Organise promotional teams and below-the-line activity for product launches, offers and customer engagement.
- **On-ground Branding & Marketing Activities:** Bring outlet branding, market visits and customer interactions into one coordinated execution plan.
- **Customized Brand Activation Services:** Shape a combination of activities around your business requirement, venue, timeline and audience.
- **Product sampling:** Organise sampling activities that introduce your product to the intended audience.
- **Product demonstration:** Help customers understand your product through guided, practical demonstrations.
- **Mall activation:** Plan promotional teams and customer engagement activity for agreed mall locations.
- **College activation:** Coordinate campus-focused promotions around the audience and campaign brief.
- **Roadshows:** Support a series of on-ground promotional activities across selected locations.
- **Events:** Arrange people and promotional support for your event's defined activities.
- **Consumer engagement:** Build direct product and brand conversations into the campaign experience.

## Planning guidance

**Give the campaign a clear starting point.** An effective activation brief connects the audience, the place and the activity.

- **Audience & message:** Define who you want to reach and what they should understand about your product.
- **Venues & dates:** Identify the campaign locations, schedule and venue arrangements required.
- **People & activities:** Agree promoter roles, product preparation and the activities to be delivered.

## Delivery process

Turn the brief into a practical plan for the team and venue.

1. **Brief:** Confirm your audience, message, venues and campaign goals.
2. **Plan:** Agree the activity, team requirement and delivery schedule.
3. **Activate:** Prepare and deploy the people carrying out the campaign.
4. **Review:** Review activity updates and discuss the campaign's next steps.
