# Promoters Services

## What ZOBHUNGER provides

Put people behind your product in stores, at events and across campaigns. Build a promoter team around the audience, venue and duration of your activation.

**Best for:** Brands planning product demonstrations, in-store promotions or an event campaign.

- **Settings:** Stores, events & campaign venues
- **Engagement:** Daily, weekly, monthly or campaign-based
- **Team focus:** Product knowledge & customer interaction

## Service scope

Choose the promoter role that matches your product and the kind of customer interaction you need.

- **Sales promoters:** Support product conversations and purchase decisions at the point of sale.
- **Brand promoters:** Introduce your brand and communicate its proposition to the intended audience.
- **Product demonstrators:** Explain product features and demonstrate how the product works.
- **Brand ambassadors:** Represent your brand with a consistent message across agreed customer interactions.
- **Event promoters:** Support visitor engagement, product introductions and promotional activities at events.
- **In-store promoters:** Assist shoppers with product information and demonstrations inside retail stores.

## Planning guidance

**A team for the duration you need.** Match the engagement to your store schedule, promotion window or campaign calendar.

- **Daily:** Plan promoter support for a specific day, event or short activity.
- **Weekly:** Cover a week of promotions or a defined run of store activities.
- **Monthly:** Build continuity for a longer in-store or promotional requirement.
- **Campaign-based:** Coordinate the team around the start, end and scope of a campaign.

## Delivery process

Prepare the role, message and venue requirements before the team is deployed.

1. **Brief:** Define your product, audience, locations and campaign dates.
2. **Select:** Source and screen promoters suited to the assignment.
3. **Prepare:** Coordinate product information and the customer interaction brief.
4. **Deploy:** Assign the team to the agreed stores or campaign venues.
5. **Review:** Review campaign activity and the updates agreed in your brief.
