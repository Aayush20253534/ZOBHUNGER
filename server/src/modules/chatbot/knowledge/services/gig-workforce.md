# Gig Workforce Services

## What ZOBHUNGER provides

Build a team around a short assignment, seasonal requirement or project. Define the work, location and duration, then plan the workforce your assignment needs.

**Best for:** Businesses planning short activities, seasonal peaks or work with a defined start and finish.

- **Duration:** A day, a week, a month or a project
- **Role types:** Promoters, field, sales & operations
- **Planning focus:** Tasks, locations & assignment dates

## Service scope

Choose the people you need for the work, then set the scope and engagement period.

- **Promoters:** Arrange people for product introductions, demonstrations and short promotional activities.
- **Sales executives:** Support a defined sales activity, outreach requirement or market assignment.
- **Field executives:** Build a team for on-ground tasks, visits and location-based assignments.
- **Telecallers:** Add calling capacity for a campaign, outreach run or follow-up requirement.
- **Recruiters:** Support sourcing, screening and recruitment coordination for a defined hiring requirement.
- **Operations staff:** Add people for project work, seasonal workload and agreed operational tasks.

## Planning guidance

**Start with how long you need the team.** Set the assignment dates and workload so the engagement can be planned around them.

- **One day:** Staff a defined activity, visit programme or single-day event.
- **One week:** Cover a short run of tasks or a week-long campaign requirement.
- **One month:** Plan workforce support for a month of project or campaign work.
- **Seasonal work:** Prepare additional capacity for a defined period of seasonal demand.
- **Project-based work:** Build the engagement around the scope and schedule of a specific project.

## Delivery process

A short assignment still needs clear roles, preparation and reporting expectations.

1. **Define:** Share the tasks, headcount, locations and assignment dates.
2. **Match:** Source candidates and review their suitability and availability.
3. **Deploy:** Brief the selected people and coordinate the assignment start.
4. **Review:** Review completed activity and any continuing workforce needs.

## Relevant industries

Retail, E-Commerce, FMCG, Logistics, Food & Beverage, Startups

## Related ZOBHUNGER services

Promoters, Operations

## How to start

Share the assignment, number of people, location and dates. We’ll help you plan the requirement. The website action for this service is **Find workforce now**.
