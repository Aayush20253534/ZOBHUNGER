# Sales Force Services

## What ZOBHUNGER provides

Build a field sales, telesales or business development team around your market. Define the territory, customer and sales activity, then put the right roles in place.

**Best for:** Businesses entering a market, expanding sales coverage or building a dedicated outreach team.

- **Sales channels:** Field, telephone & business outreach
- **Team structure:** Executives, officers & territory teams
- **Planning focus:** Market, territory & activity goals

## Service scope

Choose the team you need for prospecting, customer conversations and field execution.

- **Field sales executives:** Support customer visits, outlet coverage and sales activity in assigned locations.
- **Sales officers:** Recruit people to coordinate day-to-day sales responsibilities in your market.
- **Business development executives:** Build outreach capacity for prospecting, new business conversations and follow-ups.
- **Territory sales teams:** Organise sales roles around defined markets, territories and customer segments.
- **Telesales teams:** Coordinate product conversations and sales follow-ups through telephone outreach.
- **Telecalling teams:** Support structured calling, enquiry handling and customer follow-up activities.
- **Lead generation teams:** Build a team for prospect research, outreach and lead qualification against your brief.

## Planning guidance

**Define the market before building the team.** A useful sales brief explains who the team will speak to and what they need to accomplish.

- **Your customers:** Identify the customer segments, products and conversations the team will handle.
- **Your coverage:** Set the locations, territories and channels that need sales support.
- **Your activities:** Agree the outreach tasks, training needs and reporting expectations for the assignment.

## Delivery process

Build the role, prepare the team and review the work against agreed activities.

1. **Hire:** Source and screen candidates for the sales roles you need.
2. **Train:** Coordinate preparation around your product, customer and sales approach.
3. **Deploy:** Assign the team to the agreed territories and channels.
4. **Track:** Review activity and follow-up against the project plan.
5. **Perform:** Discuss progress, support needs and the next priorities.
