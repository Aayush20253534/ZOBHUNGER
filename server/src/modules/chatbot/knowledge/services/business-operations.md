# Operations Services

## What ZOBHUNGER provides

Create the capacity your day-to-day work needs. Build teams for outreach, customer support, data work and back-office operations, including roles suited to remote delivery.

**Best for:** Businesses building support capacity, running an outreach programme or setting up remote operations.

- **Functions:** Outreach, support & back-office work
- **Team options:** Dedicated roles & remote teams
- **Planning focus:** Workflow, workload & responsibilities

## Service scope

Choose the functions you need and define how the team will fit into your workflow.

- **Content moderation:** Review content against your approved guidelines, flag exceptions and coordinate the agreed escalation workflow.
- **Onboarding & KYC support:** Help collect onboarding information, coordinate required documents and follow up on incomplete submissions.
- **Hyperlocal business operations:** Coordinate local outreach, field follow-ups and operational tasks around your locations and business needs.
- **Lead generation:** Support prospect research, outreach and lead qualification against your requirements.
- **Telecalling:** Build a team for structured calling, enquiry handling and scheduled follow-ups.
- **Telesales:** Support sales conversations and product follow-ups through telephone outreach.
- **Customer support:** Recruit people to handle agreed customer queries and support workflows.
- **Data collection:** Gather the records, responses or information defined in your operational brief.
- **Data verification:** Review submitted information against the checks and criteria your process requires.
- **Back office support:** Build capacity for administrative tasks, record handling and routine operations.
- **Remote recruitment teams:** Coordinate remote roles for sourcing, screening and recruitment follow-ups.
- **Remote sales teams:** Build remote capacity for prospecting, sales conversations and customer follow-up.

## Planning guidance

**Fit the team into your workflow.** Define the responsibilities and hand-offs before you add operational capacity.

- **Work scope:** Describe the tasks, expected workload and responsibilities the team will own.
- **Ways of working:** Agree working hours, location needs and the tools or information required for the role.
- **Review & hand-offs:** Set the update frequency, quality checks and escalation points for the work.

## Delivery process

Build the operating brief first, then prepare the people who will carry it out.

1. **Scope:** Define the function, workload and team requirement.
2. **Hire:** Source and screen candidates for the agreed responsibilities.
3. **Onboard:** Coordinate the workflow, task instructions and required preparation.
4. **Coordinate:** Support the team's day-to-day work and agreed hand-offs.
5. **Review:** Review activity, quality checks and changing workload needs.
