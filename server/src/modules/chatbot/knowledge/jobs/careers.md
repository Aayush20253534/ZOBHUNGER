# Careers at ZOBHUNGER

The Careers page covers internal career paths across operations, sales and business development, recruitment and people operations, marketing and brand growth, technology and product, and client/execution support.

## Career areas

- **Operations:** plan, coordinate and deliver workforce and field execution.
- **Sales & business development:** build client relationships, understand requirements and turn opportunities into executable programs.
- **Recruitment & people operations:** source, screen, onboard and coordinate workforce readiness.
- **Marketing & brand growth:** campaigns, communication and market-facing initiatives.
- **Technology & product:** build and improve digital systems supporting workforce, operations, reporting and platform experiences.
- **Client & execution support:** communication, coordination, issue resolution and follow-up across businesses and execution teams.

## Working principles

The page highlights taking ownership, learning through execution, working across teams and staying close to the market.

## Hiring journey

1. Role fit.
2. Focused conversation about expectations, responsibilities and working style.
3. Capability review, which may include a practical discussion, work sample or role-relevant assessment.
4. Joining and onboarding for selected candidates.

Visitors can submit a profile at `/careers/apply` or browse work opportunities at `/jobs`.
