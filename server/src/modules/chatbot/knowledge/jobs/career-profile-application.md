# Submit a Career Profile

The public career profile form is used to submit information for ZOBHUNGER review, verification and project-fit assessment. Freshers are welcome. Submission does not guarantee selection or immediate work.

## Personal information
- Full name, email, phone, current city and state/region.
- Role or function of interest.

## Education
At least one education entry can include qualification/degree, school/college/institution, field of study and completion or expected year.

## Work experience
- Completed years of experience, including zero for a fresher.
- Optional work-experience entries can include company, role/title, start month, end month/current-work status and responsibilities/achievements.

## Skills and preferences
- Skills, entered as comma-separated items.
- Preferred locations, optional.
- Availability or notice period.
- Optional LinkedIn/portfolio URL.
- Optional note for HR covering achievements, certifications, languages or preferred work.

## Resume and privacy
A PDF resume is optional and must be 2 MB or smaller. The form asks for consent to store the profile/resume for recruitment review and to contact the person about relevant opportunities. The page specifically asks candidates to keep identity documents, bank details and other sensitive information out of the resume.
